# Play Store listing text

## App name (30 max)

Lyro: Offline Music Player

## Short description (80 max)

A clean offline player for the music on your phone. No accounts, no tracking.

## Full description (4000 max)

Lyro plays the music stored on your phone. There's no account to create, no subscription and no ads. Open it, and your library is there.

The interface is calm and simple: black and white, dot-matrix type and one red accent.

YOUR LIBRARY
• Browse songs, albums, artists and folders
• Search your whole library instantly
• Create playlists and mark favourites
• Smart lists for most played, recently added and forgotten tracks

PLAYBACK
• Plays FLAC, WAV, MP3, AAC, M4A and OPUS files
• Gapless playback, or soft fades between tracks
• Sleep timer, shuffle and repeat
• Keeps playing in the background, with lock screen and headset controls

EQUALIZER FOR YOUR EARPHONES
• Pick your IEM or earphones and apply a measured correction curve
• Fine-tune the sound with a 10-band manual equalizer
• Search thousands of models from the AutoEq catalogue

LYRICS AND ARTWORK
• Shows lyrics from .lrc files and song tags
• Optional online lookup for missing covers and lyrics, which you can turn off at any time

WEB STREAM
Listen on your laptop to the music stored on your phone. Turn on Web stream, open the address in a browser on the same Wi-Fi, and play your library there. The original files are sent as they are, nothing goes over the internet, and access is protected by a key.

HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
Now playing, disc, bar and quick mix widgets put your music one tap away.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Lyro has no accounts, ads or analytics. Your music stays on your phone.
