# Lyro privacy policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Lyro is an offline music player made by DoyaLabs. It has no accounts, no ads and no
analytics, and DoyaLabs does not collect or receive any data from the app.

## What stays on your phone

- **Your music files.** Lyro reads the audio files on your device (the "Music and audio"
  permission) to list and play them. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Your settings and activity.** Favourites, playlists, play counts, your profile name and
  avatar, equalizer settings and theme are stored only on your device and deleted when
  you uninstall Lyro.

## Optional online features

These are the only times Lyro uses the internet. Each request goes straight from your
phone to the service named below. DoyaLabs never sees it.

| Feature | Sent | To |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Missing album covers | Song title and artist | Apple's iTunes Search API (`itunes.apple.com`) |
| Lyrics | Song title, artist, album and length | LRCLIB (`lrclib.net`) |
| Equalizer profiles (when you search for your earphones) | Nothing personal; downloads the public catalogue | GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) |

Like any internet request, these services can see your IP address. Their own privacy
policies apply. You can turn online covers and lyrics off at any time in
**Profile → Online artwork & lyrics**, and Lyro then makes no requests for them.

## Web stream

When you turn on **Profile → Web stream**, Lyro runs a small web server on your phone so a
browser on the **same Wi-Fi network** can play your music. Nothing goes over the internet.
Access needs the key shown in the app, and you can change the key or turn web stream off
at any time.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|:--|:--|
| Music and audio | Find and play the songs on your device |
| Notifications | Playback controls and the "Web stream on" notice |
| Internet, network state | Optional covers, lyrics and equalizer profiles, and web stream |
| Foreground service, wake lock | Keep music and web stream running with the screen off |

## Children

Lyro is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
